# SPL3 Open Credit Management System — Flagship Upgrade Scope

---

## 🎓 Automated Degree Audit & Graduation Clearance Tracker

### 1. Overview & Objective
Transform the Student Portal and Admin Workspace with an automated **Degree Audit & Graduation Clearance Engine** that evaluates student academic progress against the BSSE / MIT curriculum requirements in real time.

---

### 2. Core Capabilities

#### A. Real-Time Degree Progress & Credit Breakdown
* **Total Credits Progress Bar / Radial Ring**:
  * Tracks total earned credits against degree requirements (e.g. `126 / 144 Credits Earned`).
* **Category-Wise Credit Counters**:
  * **Core Computer Science / SE Courses**: Checkbox list & percentage completion (e.g. `80 / 80 Credits — 100% Complete`).
  * **Technical Electives / Tracks**: Track required elective credits (e.g. `18 / 24 Credits — 2 courses remaining`).
  * **General Education / Basic Sciences**: Verification of mandatory non-CS prerequisites.
  * **Non-Credit Capstone / Internship**: Verification of SPL project and 6-month industry internship status.

#### B. Academic Standing & Clearance Verification
* **CGPA Graduation Threshold Validator**:
  * Verifies that the cumulative GPA satisfies the minimum university graduation threshold (e.g. minimum `2.50` required; displays current CGPA with color-coded safety margins).
* **Failing Grades & Incomplete Audit**:
  * Scans transcript history to ensure no active `F` (Fail) or `I` (Incomplete) grades remain unretaken.
* **Automated Clearance Status Badge**:
  * Dynamic status indicator:
    * 🟡 **In Progress — Academic Career Active** (Lists remaining courses needed).
    * 🟢 **Eligible for Degree Clearance** (All academic criteria satisfied).

#### C. Student & Admin Views
* **Student Dashboard**: An interactive audit card showing the student their exact graduation readiness and a personalized checklist of remaining requirements.
* **Admin Verification Hub**: Department administrators can view graduating batch readiness, filter eligible candidates, and generate official graduation clearance reports with one click.
